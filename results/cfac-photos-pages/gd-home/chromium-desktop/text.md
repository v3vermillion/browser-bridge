OUR MISSION

Caring for a Cause Supportive Services is a local nonprofit organization established in 2015. We are dedicated to creating and managing programs that tangibly support and enhance, the physical and emotional well being of financially disadvantaged families in Central Indiana who are facing socioeconomic hardship.

HOLIDAY ASSISTANCE OUTREACH PROGRAMS

We are partnered with Toys for Tots and would love to partner with you. We work yearly to gain sponsors and donations for low-income families in need. If you are interested in sponsoring families for one or all the programs, please feel free to contact us. During the holiday season, low-income families can get free toys, clothes, gift cards, meals, or present options from the families wish-list. We are in support of households with children, senior citizens, however anyone that is currently struggling can apply for assistance.

Our goal is to make sure we serve households with children with gifts as well as boxes of food for the entire family.  

Millions of families are struggling, and the holidays make those challenges more apparent. The goal is to ensure that every low income family has both food and some type of gift given to them.

It is highly important to us to be able to provide gifts along with dinner for the family. 

Our holiday assistance programs are the following:

- Thanksgiving 

- Christmas 

- Easter 




FREE HAIR CUT & STYLES

Our free haircut and style program aims to reach homeless and low-income disadvantaged families in need. Our goal is to build a community of skilled hairdresser volunteers who are open and willing to give their time free of charge to give haircuts, styles, make-up and face-painting to homeless and low-income families in need. 

Homeless individuals and low-income families have many challenges to face, one of them often being low-self-esteem. A haircut or style has a great effect on someone, giving them a real boost. Often times a new hair style is on the bottom of the list for those that are in need and struggling financially. One of people’s basic rights is for them to feel and look good. Remember it may be a hair-cut or style to you, but to someone in need it’s a kind gesture and you have given them hope.

If you are a Barber, Make-Up Artist, Stylist or Face Painter and want to partner and donate your time. Please contact us!
