DIAPER DRIVE
INITIATIVE PROGRAM

Our diaper pantry program is entitled, “Diaper Drive Initiative Program.” Our Diaper Drive Initiative program is committed to connecting those in need with a reliable supply of diapers and other needed resources.

We provide diapers monthly as part of our on-going services and programs. We also provide partnering organizations information as a resource for the family in need.

HOLIDAY ASSISTANCE
OUTREACH PROGRAMS

We work yearly to gain sponsors and donations for low-income families in need. If you are interested in sponsoring families for one or all the programs, please feel free to contact us. During the holiday season, low-income families can get free toys, clothes, gift cards, meals, or present options from the families wish-list. We are in support of households with children, senior citizens, however anyone that is currently struggling can apply for assistance.

FREE HAIR
CUT & STYLES

Our free haircut and style program aims to reach homeless and low-income disadvantaged families in need. Our goal is to build a community of skilled hairdresser volunteers who are open and willing to give their time free of charge to give haircuts, styles, make-up and face-painting to homeless and low-income families in need.

Homeless individuals and low-income families have many challenges to face, one of them often being low-self-esteem.

HISTORY

Seeing a need for energetic, nonprofit work in this area, we formed our organization to provide sensible solutions. We've consistently grown since then, all thanks to the helping hands of this amazing community!


MISSION

We are dedicated to creating and managing programs that tangibly support and enhance, the physical and emotional well being of financially disadvantaged families in Central Indiana who are facing socioeconomic hardship.
