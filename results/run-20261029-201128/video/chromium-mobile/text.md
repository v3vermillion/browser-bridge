THANKSGIVING, CHRISTMAS, EASTER
Holiday Assistance

Food boxes and gifts, so every household has a holiday meal and something to open.

WHO IT'S FOR
Low-income families in Central Indiana, especially households with children.

Families can receive toys, clothes, gift cards, meals, or gifts from their wish list.
Sponsors choose a family and help fill its holiday needs.
We partner with Toys for Tots.

ABOUT
Meet Tamara Long-Ajimati

Tamara founded Caring for a Cause in 2015 to support families in Central Indiana who are facing financial hardship. More than a decade on, it has helped over 5,000 families.

Its part-time volunteers are committed to helping others, and the organization has grown thanks to the helping hands of this community.
