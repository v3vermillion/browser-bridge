THANKSGIVING, CHRISTMAS, EASTER
Holiday Assistance

Food boxes and gifts, so every household has a holiday meal and something to open.

Families can receive toys, clothes, gift cards, meals, or gifts from their wish list.

ABOUT
Meet Tamara Long-Ajimati

Tamara founded Caring for a Cause in 2015 to support families in Central Indiana who are facing financial hardship. Year after year since then, she and her volunteers have helped more than 5,000 families.

Her part-time volunteers are committed to helping others, and the organization has grown thanks to the helping hands of this community.
